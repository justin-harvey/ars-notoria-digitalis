# Book of Mirrors - Complete Guide

## What Is the Book of Mirrors? 🪞

The **Book of Mirrors** is your **personal magical journal** for recording spiritual experiences, insights, ritual results, and inner reflections. In occult tradition, a "mirror" reflects truth - both what you show to the world and what you hide from yourself.

### The Concept Behind the Name

**"Book of Mirrors"** represents:

1. **Self-Reflection** - Mirror work is a powerful psychological and magical practice
2. **Truth Revelation** - Mirrors show reality without filter
3. **Shadow Work** - Seeing and integrating your hidden self
4. **Magical Record** - Documenting your spiritual journey
5. **Scrying Tool** - Mirrors are used in divination to see beyond the veil

This is your **magical diary**, but deeper than a normal journal - it's where you confront yourself honestly and track your spiritual evolution.

## How to Use the Book of Mirrors Effectively 📖

### **Daily Practice:**

1. **Morning Review** - Read yesterday's entry, note patterns
2. **Evening Recording** - Document the day's magical work
3. **Weekly Reflection** - Review week's entries, identify themes
4. **Monthly Summary** - Track lunar cycle influences

### **Tag Strategy:**

- Use 3-5 tags per entry
- Be consistent with tag names (`new-moon` not `newmoon`)
- Create a "master tag list" in a separate entry
- Review tags monthly, consolidate similar ones

### **Writing Tips:**

**DO:**
- ✅ Write immediately after experiences (while fresh)
- ✅ Be brutally honest (especially in shadow work)
- ✅ Include sensory details (sights, sounds, feelings)
- ✅ Note time, date, moon phase, planetary hour
- ✅ Record "failures" - they teach the most!

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Censor yourself (this is YOUR mirror)
- ❌ Wait too long (memories fade)
- ❌ Skip "boring" days (patterns emerge over time)
- ❌ Edit for others (no one else will read this)

## Privacy & Security 🔒

**Your Book of Mirrors is stored in browser localStorage:**

✅ **Private** - Only accessible on your device
✅ **Not uploaded** - Never sent to servers
✅ **Persistent** - Survives page refreshes
❌ **Not backed up** - Use Export feature!

### **Backup Your Entries:**

Currently, the grimoire has export functionality. To backup:

1. Use browser DevTools (F12)
2. Console tab
3. Run: `console.log(localStorage.getItem('grimoire_data'))`
4. Copy the output to a text file
5. Store securely (encrypted folder, password-protected)

**Pro tip:** Keep a physical backup in a locked notebook. Digital is convenient, physical is secure.

## Philosophical Foundation 🪞

### Why It's Called "Book of MIRRORS"

In occult tradition, **mirror work** is profound:

1. **Scrying Mirrors** (Black mirrors, obsidian) - Used for divination
2. **Mirror Gazing** - Confronting the self, seeing beyond ego
3. **Reflection** - "As above, so below" - the outer mirrors the inner
4. **Truth** - Mirrors don't lie, they show what IS
5. **Shadow Integration** - Seeing the parts you hide

**Your journal is a mirror** - it reflects your true spiritual progress, stripped of ego and illusion.

### Traditional Mirror Work

You can COMBINE mirror gazing with journaling:

**Exercise:**
1. Light candle in dark room
2. Sit before mirror
3. Gaze into your own eyes for 10-15 minutes
4. Don't look away, even when face shifts
5. Immediately journal what you saw/felt
6. Tag: `mirror-gazing`, `shadow-work`, `self`

This is intense practice but extremely revealing.
