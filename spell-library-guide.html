Spell Difficulty Levels

  • Beginner - Simple, safe, requires minimal tools. Good for daily practice.
  • Intermediate - Requires more preparation, specific timing, or ritual setup.
  • Advanced - Complex operations requiring experience and deep knowledge.

Start with beginner spells and work your way up!

Moon Phase Guide

Spells are most effective when performed during the appropriate lunar phase:

  • New Moon - New beginnings, fresh starts, manifestation
  • Waxing Moon - Growth, building power, attracting
  • Full Moon - Maximum power, completion, charging
  • Waning Moon - Banishing, releasing, cleansing
  • Dark Moon - Deep shadow work, divination, necromancy

Safety & Ethics

⚠️ Important Reminders:
  1. Always ground and center before and after magical work
  2. Cleanse your space (use LBRP or salt water)
  3. Respect free will - never manipulate others
  4. Protection first - especially for spirit work
  5. Keep records - document results in the grimoire
  6. Start small - master basics before attempting advanced work
🛑 Warning Signs to Stop:
  • Extreme fatigue
  • Persistent negative experiences
  • Physical illness after ritual
  • Unwanted manifestations
  • Loss of control

If you experience any of these, perform banishing and take a break from practice.
